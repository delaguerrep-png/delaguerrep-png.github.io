This policy explains how Lantern Light Studio ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our YouTube channels and our internal publishing tool, Story Studio.

1. Who we are

Lantern Light Studio is an independent media studio based in South Africa. Contact: delaguerrep@gmail.com.

2. Story Studio and the YouTube API

Story Studio is a private application used only by us to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By using our channels you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

When we authorise Story Studio with our own Google account, it receives an access token limited to these permissions: uploading videos, and managing the thumbnails, captions and playlists of our own channels. Story Studio does not access, collect, store or share any data belonging to viewers or to any other YouTube user. It does not read comments, subscriber lists, watch history or analytics about individuals.

The authorisation token is stored encrypted as a secret in our private build system and is used solely to upload our content. We can revoke this access at any time at Google security settings, and the token is deleted when the tool is retired.

2b. Story Studio and Facebook

Story Studio also posts our own videos to our own Facebook Pages using the Meta Pages API. It only uses permission to publish and manage posts on Pages we own. It does not access, collect or store data about people who view or follow our Pages.

3. Viewers of our channels

We do not collect personal information from viewers. YouTube may collect information when you watch, like, comment or subscribe; that is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as total views) are shown to us by YouTube Studio.

4. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share it. You may ask us to delete our correspondence with you at any time.

5. Children

Our channels are made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa and similar laws, you may ask what personal information we hold about you and request its correction or deletion. Contact us at the address above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
