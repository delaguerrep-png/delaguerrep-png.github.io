About us

Lantern Light Studio produces narrated story videos for YouTube, beginning with Lantern Light Bible Stories — reverent, cinematic retellings of the great stories of the Bible for adults and families around the world.

Each episode is researched and written with care to stay faithful to the source text. We use modern production tools, including AI-assisted narration and illustration, and every video is reviewed by a person before it is published. Videos that contain synthetic media are labelled as such on YouTube.

Story Studio

Story Studio is our private, internal publishing tool. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own finished videos, thumbnails and captions to our own YouTube channels on a schedule. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's data.
