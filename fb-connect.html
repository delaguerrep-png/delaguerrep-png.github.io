Story Studio – connect Facebook Pages

Private setup page. Everything on this page happens only inside your own browser — nothing is sent to or stored on this website.

Step 1 · Allow Story Studio to post to your Pages

Click the button, choose both Pages and approve. Facebook brings you back here.

Step 2 · Paste the App Secret

Open Story Studio → App settings → Basic, click Show next to App secret, copy it and paste it here. It is used once, in this browser only, to make the posting codes permanent.

Step 3 · Copy each code into GitHub

For each Page: click Copy, then click Open GitHub and paste it as the secret value, then Add secret.

These codes do not expire. You can cancel them any time in Facebook → Settings → Business integrations.
